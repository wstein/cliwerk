Migration from picocli

A guide for moving from picocli to cliwerk will be added here.

The user manual and quick guide are inherited from upstream picocli and are covered by the attribution in NOTICE. Where they say info.picocli:picocli or picocli.info, the cliwerk coordinates are io.github.wstein:cliwerk and this site.
